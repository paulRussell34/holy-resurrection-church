First Visit

What to Expect

If you have never been to an Orthodox service before, here is a brief guide to help you feel at home.

The Service Is Sung

Orthodox worship is entirely sung or chanted - there is no organ or piano. The human voice is the instrument of the Church. You do not need to sing along; simply listen and take it in.

Icons and Prayer

You will see icons throughout the church. The faithful bow and make the sign of the cross at various points in the service. Visitors are welcome to observe without participating in these gestures.

Holy Communion

Holy Communion is reserved for baptized and chrismated Orthodox Christians who have prepared through prayer and fasting. Non-Orthodox visitors are welcome to receive a blessing from the priest during communion.

Dress and Conduct

Modest dress is appreciated, though there is no strict requirement for first-time visitors. Children are welcome and encouraged to be present for the liturgy. We are a small community, so you will not be lost in a crowd.
