About Us

Our Faith & Our Community

Who We Are

A Growing Mission Parish

Holy Resurrection Orthodox Church is a mission outreach of the Ukrainian Orthodox Church of the USA (UOC-USA), under the omophorion of the Ecumenical Patriarchate of Constantinople. We are a small, growing parish in the mountains of western North Carolina.

We gather week by week to pray together, support one another, and share the ancient faith with our neighbors in Haywood County and the surrounding region. Our community welcomes people from all backgrounds who are curious about Orthodox Christianity.

Our mission is captured in a single sentence from St. John Chrysostom: the Church is a hospital for souls, not a courtroom. We are here for people who are searching, hurting, or simply hungry for something more.

Theology

Our Faith

Orthodox Christianity is not merely a set of beliefs to be agreed to -- it is a way of life to be lived. The Church calls each person into a deeper knowledge of God through worship, prayer, fasting, and the sacraments, drawing on a tradition that reaches back to the apostles themselves.

Apostolic Continuity

Orthodox Christianity has maintained an unbroken succession from the apostles through the bishops of the Church. We hold to the same faith proclaimed at the seven Ecumenical Councils.

Liturgical Worship

Our worship is sung entirely by the human voice - no instruments, no amplification. The Divine Liturgy is a participation in the eternal worship of God, drawing on centuries of hymnody and prayer.

Theosis

Orthodoxy understands salvation as more than forgiveness. It is a process of becoming like God - a union with God's life and love that begins here and continues into eternity.

Holy Icons

Icons are not idols but windows into the eternal. They depict Christ, the Theotokos (Mary, Mother of God), and the saints, and are venerated as a sign of honor for the persons they represent.

Holy Scripture & Tradition

Orthodox Christians read Scripture as part of the living Tradition of the Church. The two are not in competition - the Church both produced the New Testament and continues to interpret it.

The Resurrection

Everything in Orthodox faith centers on the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ. Our very parish name - Holy Resurrection - proclaims what we believe at the heart of our worship and our lives.
