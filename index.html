Welcome

Come and Worship with Us

Whether you are Orthodox, exploring Christianity for the first time, or simply looking for a community grounded in ancient faith, you are welcome here. Our doors are open.

Ancient Faith

Orthodox Christianity traces an unbroken line to the apostles and the early Church. Our worship, theology, and way of life are rooted in that continuous tradition.

All Are Welcome

Wherever you are in your journey, you are welcome to attend, observe, and ask questions. We are glad to have you with us.

Local Community

We gather in the heart of Waynesville, NC as a small and growing mission parish. Come and see what the Orthodox faith looks like up close.
